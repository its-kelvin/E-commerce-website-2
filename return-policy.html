Return Policy

Hassle-free returns within 14 days

Return Eligibility

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. You may return items under the following conditions:

Eligible for Return

  • Items received damaged or defective
  • Wrong item delivered
  • Items not matching the description
  • Change of mind (within 14 days, unused condition)

Not Eligible for Return

  • Items used, washed, or worn
  • Items missing original packaging or tags
  • Perishable goods
  • Software, digital products, or gift cards
  • Personalized/customized items
  • Items marked as "Final Sale"

Return Process

Step 1: Request a Return

Contact our customer service within 14 days of receiving your order:

  • Email: returns@crucialonlinesales.co.ke
  • Phone: +254 700 123 456
  • Include your order number and reason for return

Step 2: Approval

Our team will review your request and respond within 24-48 hours with:

  • Return authorization number
  • Return shipping instructions
  • Return shipping label (for defective/wrong items)

Step 3: Ship the Item

  • Pack the item securely in original packaging
  • Include all accessories, manuals, and tags
  • Attach the return shipping label
  • Drop off at designated courier location

Step 4: Inspection & Refund

Once we receive your return:

  • We inspect the item within 2 business days
  • Refund is processed to your original payment method
  • You receive confirmation via email/SMS

Refund Information

Refund Methods

  • M-Pesa: Refund within 24-48 hours
  • Card Payments: Refund within 5-10 business days
  • Store Credit: Immediate (for exchanges)

Refund Amounts

  • Full refund for defective/wrong items (including return shipping)
  • Full refund for change of mind (original shipping not refunded)
  • Partial refund may apply if item is not in original condition

Late or Missing Refunds

If you haven't received your refund:

  1. Check your bank account/M-Pesa again
  2. Contact your bank/credit card company
  3. If still missing, contact us at returns@crucialonlinesales.co.ke

Exchanges

We offer exchanges for:

  • Different sizes (clothing, shoes)
  • Different colors (if available)
  • Defective replacements

Note: Exchanges are subject to stock availability. If the requested item is unavailable, we will issue a refund instead.

Warranty Information

Many of our products come with manufacturer warranties:

  • Electronics: 6-24 months
  • Appliances: 12 months
  • Phones: 12 months

Warranty claims are handled directly with manufacturers or through our service center.
