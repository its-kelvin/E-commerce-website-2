Delivery Policy

Fast, reliable delivery across Kenya

Delivery Options

We offer multiple delivery options to suit your needs:

Standard Delivery (2-5 Business Days)

  • Within Nairobi: KSH 300
  • Outside Nairobi (Major towns): KSH 500
  • Remote areas: KSH 700

Express Delivery (Same Day / Next Day)

  • Nairobi only: KSH 600
  • Orders placed before 12 PM eligible for same-day delivery

Free Delivery

  • Orders above KSH 10,000 qualify for free standard delivery
  • Free delivery applies to Nairobi and major towns only

Delivery Process

Order Processing

All orders are processed within 24 hours of payment confirmation. You will receive an SMS and email confirmation once your order is dispatched.

Tracking Your Order

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking number via SMS. You can use this to track your delivery status on our website or contact our customer service.

Delivery Partners

We partner with trusted courier services including:

  • G4S Kenya
  • Speedaf Express
  • Bolt Business (for express deliveries)
  • In-house delivery team (Nairobi area)

Important Information

Delivery Address

Please ensure your delivery address is accurate and complete. Include:

  • County
  • Town/City
  • Street name and building number
  • Landmark (if applicable)
  • Contact phone number

Failed Delivery Attempts

If delivery fails due to incorrect address or unavailability, we will:

  • Contact you to reschedule delivery
  • Hold your package for up to 7 days
  • Return to sender after 7 days (redelivery fees apply)

Inspection Upon Delivery

We encourage all customers to inspect their packages before accepting delivery. Report any damage immediately to the delivery personnel.
