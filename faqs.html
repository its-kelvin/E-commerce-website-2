Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

Ordering

How do I place an order?

Simply browse our products, add items to your cart, and proceed to checkout. You'll need to provide your delivery details and complete payment to finalize your order.

Can I modify or cancel my order?

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 2 hours of placement. Contact our customer service team immediately at +254 700 123 456 or email support@crucialonlinesales.co.ke.

Do I need an account to order?

No, you can checkout as a guest. However, creating an account allows you to track orders, save addresses, and receive exclusive offers.

How do I know my order was successful?

You will receive an order confirmation via email and SMS within minutes of placing your order. If you don't receive confirmation, check your spam folder or contact us.

Payment

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept M-Pesa, credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard), and bank transfers. All payments are processed securely through Paystack.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes! We use industry-standard SSL encryption and PCI-compliant payment processors. We never store your full card details on our servers.

Can I pay on delivery?

Currently, we do not offer cash on delivery. All orders must be paid for in advance to ensure smooth processing and delivery.

Will I receive an invoice?

Yes, an electronic invoice will be emailed to you after your order is confirmed. You can also request a printed invoice upon delivery.

Delivery

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 2-5 business days depending on your location. Express same-day delivery is available in Nairobi for orders placed before 12 PM.

How much is shipping?

Shipping costs vary by location: Nairobi (KSH 300), major towns (KSH 500), remote areas (KSH 700). Free shipping on orders over KSH 10,000.

Do you deliver outside Kenya?

Currently, we only deliver within Kenya. We are working to expand our delivery services to neighboring countries soon.

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you will receive an SMS with a tracking number. You can use this to track your delivery status on our website or contact our customer service.

Returns & Refunds

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery for unused items in original packaging. Damaged or defective items can be returned for a full refund or exchange.

How do I return an item?

Contact our returns team at returns@crucialonlinesales.co.ke with your order number and reason for return. We'll provide a return authorization and shipping instructions.

When will I receive my refund?

M-Pesa refunds are processed within 24-48 hours. Card refunds may take 5-10 business days depending on your bank.

Can I exchange an item?

Yes, exchanges are available for different sizes or colors (subject to stock availability). Contact us to arrange an exchange.

Products

Are your products genuine?

Absolutely! We source all products directly from authorized distributors and manufacturers. Every product comes with original packaging and manufacturer warranty.

What if the product I want is out of stock?

You can sign up for restock notifications on the product page. We'll email you as soon as the item becomes available.

Do products come with warranty?

Yes, most electronics and appliances come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 6 to 24 months. Warranty details are listed on each product page.

Can I get a discount for bulk orders?

Yes! We offer special pricing for bulk orders. Contact our sales team at sales@crucialonlinesales.co.ke for a custom quote.

Account

How do I create an account?

Click on the profile icon in the top right corner and select "Register." Fill in your details and verify your email to complete registration.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. Enter your email address and we'll send you a link to reset your password.

How do I update my delivery address?

Log into your account and go to Settings. You can add, edit, or delete saved addresses from there.
